# IEEE QP Milestone Reports & Progress Tracking

This document outlines the purpose, schedule, and expectations for Quarterly Projects (QP) milestone progress reports.

---

## 1. 🎯 What is a Milestone Report?

Milestone reports are short, mandatory check-ins submitted by each team via Google Forms throughout the 10-week quarter. 

Their primary purpose is to:
1. **Track Technical Progress**: Help the QP Chairs and Mentors see what each team has built, wired, soldered, or coded.
2. **Surface Blockers Early**: Identify teams stuck on technical issues (circuit bugs, firmware errors, missing parts) before it's too late.
3. **Connect Teams with Mentors**: Ensure struggling teams receive hands-on assistance during Workathons, Open Access Hours, or in their private Discord help channels.
4. **Monitor Team Health**: Keep tabs on teammate communication and participation.

> [!NOTE]
> Reporting difficulties, technical hurdles, or scaling down your original proposal scope will **never** negatively affect your grading. Engineering is iterative, and pivots are expected!

---

## 2. 📅 Milestone Schedule & Timeline

Quarterly Projects typically spans 10 weeks, with hands-on project work occurring between Weeks 2 and 9:

- **Week 2–3**: Project Proposal submitted and approved.
- **Milestone 1 (~Week 4)**: Early Prototyping & Parts Check.
  - Verification that required components are on hand or requested.
  - Initial circuit breadboarding and basic firmware/sensor tests.
- **Milestone 2 (~Week 6)**: Subsystem Integration & Testing.
  - Individual subsystems (e.g. power, sensors, motors, microcontroller code) begin working together.
  - Identification of critical hardware/software bugs.
- **Milestone 3 (~Week 8)**: Feature Freeze & Showcase Preparation.
  - Core functionality locked in; physical assembly and cosmetic polish.
  - Preparation for Devpost code submission and live demonstration.
- **Week 9–10**: Final Devpost Submission & In-Person Showcase.

---

## 3. 📝 What Teams Need to Submit

- **Frequency**: One submission per team (submitted by any designated team representative).
- **Core Information Required**:
  - **Milestone Number**: (Milestone 1, 2, or 3)
  - **Team Number & Title**: (e.g. Team 7 - "Smart Weather Station")
  - **Active Teammates**: Confirmation of who is actively contributing.
  - **Tangible Accomplishments**: What has been built, tested, or programmed since kickoff.
  - **Scope Changes**: Any simplifications or pivots from the original project proposal.
  - **Demo Evidence**: Link to a photo, short video clip, or GitHub repository showing the prototype in action.
  - **Blockers & Need Help**: Direct statement of any issues blocking the team or specific mentor help needed.
  - **Showcase Confidence Score**: 1 to 5 scale assessing readiness for demo day.

---

## 4. 🆘 Need Immediate Help Between Milestones?

You don't have to wait for the next milestone form if your team is stuck:
1. **Discord Mentor Queue**: Go to `#help-tickets` in the `Help Requests` category and click **"Request Mentor Help / Feedback"** to summon an available mentor to a private help channel.
2. **Ask the AI**: Post your circuit, wiring, or programming questions in `#ask-ai`.
3. **Open Access Hours**: Visit the IEEE Project Space (4th Floor Jacobs Hall) during Open Access Hours to debug with lab equipment and mentors.
