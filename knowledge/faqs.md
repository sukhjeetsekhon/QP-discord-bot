## 1. 💰 Budgets, Hardware Purchases, & Reimbursements

### What is our team budget for Fall 2026, and how do purchases work?
• **No Individual Team Budgets:** Teams do **not** have an individual budget this quarter (Fall 2026).
• **Shared Components:** The QP Chair has already allocated and spent the hardware budget purchasing shared components in bulk for all teams to use in common.
• **Out-of-Pocket Spending:** If your team requires specific components or parts not available in the shared lab stock, you will need to spend your own money (out-of-pocket). Individual purchases are not reimbursed.

### Are small API or software costs covered or reimbursed?
• **No:** API subscriptions (e.g. OpenAI, cloud services, web APIs) and software licenses are **not covered** and **cannot be reimbursed** under any circumstances.

### What happens to the remaining QP budget?
• The only remaining budget for QP is strictly reserved for the **prize funds** awarded to winning teams at the final showcase.

### How does this differ from the historical QP budget?
• Historically, QP offered a $50 base budget plus $11 per verified IEEE member. For Fall 2026, this was replaced by bulk shared parts provided directly by IEEE leadership.

## 2. 🎯 Milestones & Progress Reports

### What exactly is expected for Milestone 1 and Milestone 2?
• **Purpose:** Milestones 1 and 2 are **progress reports**, not punitive grading checkpoints.
• **Primary Goals:**
  - Provide visibility to the QP Chair and mentors on how each team is progressing.
  - Surface blockers (wiring errors, component issues, code bugs) early before they become critical.
  - Push teams to start actively working, prototyping, and meeting early rather than procrastinating until the end of the quarter.
• **Grading Policy:** Pivoting your idea, scaling down features, or reporting technical hurdles will **never** negatively affect your team. Just report your honest status.

## 5. 🔋 Batteries & Lab Safety

### Are there any rules for using or charging LiPo batteries at workathons or in the lab?
• **Competition Rules:** There are **no special QP-specific rules or bans** on using LiPo (Lithium Polymer) batteries.
• **Mandatory Safety Practices:** Any team utilizing or charging LiPo batteries must strictly follow standard lithium battery safety protocols:
  - **Never Charge Unattended:** Never leave a charging LiPo battery unattended or charging overnight.
  - **Use Proper Balance Chargers:** Always use a compatible LiPo balance charger configured to the exact cell count (e.g. 1S = 3.7V, 2S = 7.4V, 3S = 11.1V). Never charge with an unregulated DC power supply.
  - **Fire-Safe Charging:** Charge and store batteries on non-flammable surfaces (ceramic tile, metal bench) or inside a flame-retardant LiPo safety bag.
  - **Inspect Before Use:** Check for swelling, puffiness, puncture marks, or damaged wiring. Never charge or operate a swollen or physically compromised battery.
  - **Double-Check Polarity:** Always verify positive (red / VCC) and negative (black / GND) wiring before powering microcontrollers or motor drivers to prevent short circuits.
  - **Disconnect if Hot:** If a battery becomes unusually hot, emits an odor, or swells during charging or discharge, immediately disconnect power safely.
