## 1. 💰 Budgets, Hardware Purchases, & Reimbursements

### What is our team budget for Fall 2026, and how do purchases work?
• **No Individual Team Budgets:** Teams do **not** have an individual budget this quarter (Fall 2026).
• **Shared Components:** The QP Chair has already allocated and spent the hardware budget purchasing shared components in bulk for all teams to use in common.
• **Out-of-Pocket Spending:** If your team requires specific components or parts not available in the shared lab stock, you will need to spend your own money (out-of-pocket). Individual purchases are not reimbursed.

### Are small API or software costs covered or reimbursed?
• **No:** API subscriptions (e.g. OpenAI, cloud services, web APIs) and software licenses are **not covered** and **cannot be reimbursed** under any circumstances.

### What happens to the remaining QP budget?
• The only remaining budget for QP is strictly reserved for the **prize funds** awarded to winning teams at the final showcase.

### How does this differ from the historical QP budget?
• Historically, QP offered a $50 base budget plus $11 per verified IEEE member. For Fall 2026, this was replaced by bulk shared parts provided directly by IEEE leadership.

### How do we borrow lab components or tools from the Project Space?
• **Three-Channel Photo Borrowing System:**
  1. Head to the **`#borrow-items`** channel.
  2. Click the **"📸 Borrow Lab Item"** button on the panel.
  3. A private 24-hour checkout thread is opened for your team.
  4. In that thread, upload a clear **photo** of the item(s) you are borrowing along with the **item name / description & quantity** (e.g. `2x SG90 Servos, 1x Breadboard`).
  5. The bot automatically posts your active checkout card to **`#borrowed-items`** (public board showing all items currently in use) and archives an internal audit copy in **`#inventory`** (private to Leadership).
  6. When returning items to the lab during Open Access Hours, click **Request Return 📦** on your card in `#borrowed-items`. An officer will inspect the item and click **Confirm Return ✅** in `#inventory`, which automatically clears the active card from `#borrowed-items`!
  7. Use `/borrowed-items` to check items your team currently has checked out.

## 2. 🎯 Milestones & Progress Reports

### What exactly is expected for Milestone 1 and Milestone 2?
• **Purpose:** Milestones 1 and 2 are **progress reports**, not punitive grading checkpoints.
• **Primary Goals:**
  - Provide visibility to the QP Chair and mentors on how each team is progressing.
  - Surface blockers (wiring errors, component issues, code bugs) early before they become critical.
  - Push teams to start actively working, prototyping, and meeting early rather than procrastinating until the end of the quarter.
• **Grading Policy:** Pivoting your idea, scaling down features, or reporting technical hurdles will **never** negatively affect your team. Just report your honest status.

## 5. 🔋 Batteries & Lab Safety

### Are there any rules for using or charging LiPo batteries at workathons or in the lab?
• **Competition Rules:** There are **no special QP-specific rules or bans** on using LiPo (Lithium Polymer) batteries.
• **Mandatory Safety Practices:** Any team utilizing or charging LiPo batteries must strictly follow standard lithium battery safety protocols:
  - **Never Charge Unattended:** Never leave a charging LiPo battery unattended or charging overnight.
  - **Use Proper Balance Chargers:** Always use a compatible LiPo balance charger configured to the exact cell count (e.g. 1S = 3.7V, 2S = 7.4V, 3S = 11.1V). Never charge with an unregulated DC power supply.
  - **Fire-Safe Charging:** Charge and store batteries on non-flammable surfaces (ceramic tile, metal bench) or inside a flame-retardant LiPo safety bag.
  - **Inspect Before Use:** Check for swelling, puffiness, puncture marks, or damaged wiring. Never charge or operate a swollen or physically compromised battery.
  - **Double-Check Polarity:** Always verify positive (red / VCC) and negative (black / GND) wiring before powering microcontrollers or motor drivers to prevent short circuits.
  - **Disconnect if Hot:** If a battery becomes unusually hot, emits an odor, or swells during charging or discharge, immediately disconnect power safely.

## 7. 🤖 Discord Bot Commands & Self-Service Guide

### What slash commands can participants use?
• **`/my-team`:** Instant dashboard of your team roster, remaining budget, and borrowed lab items.
• **`/team-info team:`:** Look up any team's (1–28) roster, budget status, and borrowed equipment.
• **`/ask-ai question: [private:]`:** Ask the AI helper questions about competition rules, schedules, budgets, circuit schematics, microcontroller pinouts, or code debugging.
• **`/ai-status`:** Check the operational health of the AI assistant, loaded knowledge files, and synced channels.
• **`/inventory [search:] [category:]`:** Search parts and tools in the IEEE lab and find their cabinet/bin storage locations.
• **`/whereis item:`:** Check whether an item is in the lab or which teams have borrowed units.
• **`/borrow-photo image: item: [team:]`:** Directly log borrowed lab items with a photo attachment.
• **`/borrowed-items [team:] [status:]`:** View visual photo cards of borrowed items currently in use by your team or all teams.
• **`/borrowed [team:]`:** View a text list of inventory items checked out by your team or another team.
• **`/loan-history [team:] [item:] [limit:]`:** View chronological audit logs of checkouts and returns.
• **`/budget [team:]`:** Check hardware budget balance and purchase history.

### How do I join my team on Discord?
• Go to the **`#team-assignment`** channel.
• Click the **"Join Team"** button.
• In the pop-up modal, type your team number (`1` to `28`) and submit.
• You will receive your `Team <N>` role and immediate private access to `#team-<N>` and `Team <N> VC`.

### How do I borrow tools or components from the Project Space?
• Head to **`#borrow-items`** and click **"📸 Borrow Lab Item"** (or use the `/borrow-photo` command).
• A private 24-hour checkout thread opens for your team.
• Upload a photo of the item(s) and reply with the description and quantity (e.g. `2x SG90 Servos, 1x Breadboard`).
• The bot creates an active equipment card in **`#borrowed-items`** and archives a private record in **`#inventory`**.

### How do I return borrowed items?
• Bring the physical items to the IEEE Project Space (Jacobs Hall 4309) during Open Access Hours.
• Find your item's card in **`#borrowed-items`** and click **"Request Return 📦"**.
• The card status switches to `⏳ Return Pending Review` and pings `@QP Chair` in `#inventory`.
• The on-duty officer inspects the hardware and clicks **"Confirm Return ✅"**, automatically clearing your card from the active board!

### How do I get mentor help when I am stuck on a technical bug?
• Go to the **`#mentor-queue`** (or `#help-tickets`) channel.
• Click **"🙋 Request Mentor"**.
• Enter a brief description of what you need help with and where you are located (e.g. `Project Space Bench 3` or `Team 4 VC`).
• A private help ticket (`#help-X`) is opened. An IEEE mentor will claim your request and assist you!

### Why did a command tell me "Only Leadership can run this"?
• Administrative commands (such as `/inventory-add`, `/checkout`, `/return`, `/budget-adjust`, `/budget-summary`, and setup commands) are reserved for the **QP Chair**, **Officers**, and **Server Administrators**.
• Participants do not need to run these commands directly—officers use them to manage inventory stock, configure channels, and audit lab hardware.
