# IEEE Quarterly Projects (QP) — Milestone Progress Report Form Guide

This document is a complete, ready-to-use template for building your **QP Milestone Progress Report Google Form**.

---

## 📋 Form Overview & Settings

- **Form Title:** `IEEE Quarterly Projects (QP) — Milestone Progress Report`
- **Form Description:**
  ```text
  Welcome to the IEEE QP Milestone Progress Report!
  This check-in helps the QP Chairs and Mentors track your team's technical progress, identify blockers early, and provide hardware or mentorship support before the showcase.

  • Only ONE submission is required per team.
  • Please answer honestly — reporting difficulties or changes to your original proposal will NEVER hurt your standing. We are here to help you succeed!
  ```
- **Recommended Google Form Settings:**
  - **Collect Email Addresses:** Verified or Responder Input (Recommended)
  - **Limit to 1 response:** Enabled (Allow responders to edit after submit)
  - **Allow response editing:** Enabled (In case teams update their demo link or fix an answer)

## 📑 Section 2: Technical Progress & Demo Evidence

*Use this section to record technical achievements and tangible proof of work.*

---

### Question 2.1: Key Accomplishments
- **Question:** What tangible progress has your team made since kickoff / the last milestone?
- **Question Type:** `Paragraph`
- **Required:** ✅ Yes
- **Description / Helper Text:**
  ```text
  Briefly describe what your team designed, built, wired, soldered, or programmed.
  Example: "Assembled the chassis, wired the motor driver to Arduino pins 5 & 6, and wrote the initial PWM speed control code."
  ```

---

### Question 2.2: Changes from Initial Proposal
- **Question:** Have you made any changes, pivots, or simplifications from your initial Project Proposal?
- **Question Type:** `Paragraph`
- **Required:** ⚪ Optional (or Required with "None / on track")
- **Description / Helper Text:**
  ```text
  It is completely normal to scale down features or switch parts as you learn. Tell us about any scope adjustments so mentors are on the same page. If no changes, write "None".
  ```

---

### Question 2.3: Progress Demo Link (Photo, Video, or Repository)
- **Question:** Link to demo photo, video, or GitHub code repository
- **Question Type:** `Short Answer` (or `File Upload` if preferred)
- **Required:** ⚪ Optional (Highly Recommended)
- **Description / Helper Text:**
  ```text
  Provide a Google Drive, Imgur, YouTube, or GitHub link showing your prototype, schematic, or working code.
  (Make sure sharing permissions are set to "Anyone with the link can view").
  ```

---

## 📑 Section 3: Blockers & Need Help

*A simple, direct check-in so leadership can immediately step in to help.*

---

### Question 3.1: Major Blockers & Need for Mentor Help
- **Question:** Are you facing any major blockers or need mentor help?
- **Question Type:** `Paragraph`
- **Required:** ✅ Yes
- **Description / Helper Text:**
  ```text
  If you are stuck on hardware debugging, wiring, code compilation, soldering, or missing components, please explain here!
  Be specific about what is not working and what kind of mentor assistance you need. If everything is running smoothly, simply write "None".
  ```

---

## 📑 Section 4: Showcase Readiness & Next Steps

*Track overall timeline confidence and upcoming priorities.*

---

### Question 4.1: Showcase Confidence Level
- **Question:** How confident are you that your project will have a working demonstration ready for the Showcase?
- **Question Type:** `Linear Scale` (1 to 5)
- **Required:** ✅ Yes
- **Scale Labels:**
  - `1`: Critical concern / at risk of not functioning
  - `3`: On track with some uncertainties
  - `5`: Confident / ahead of schedule

---

### Question 4.2: Next Week's Goal
- **Question:** What is your team's primary goal to complete before the next milestone / check-in?
- **Question Type:** `Paragraph`
- **Required:** ✅ Yes
- **Description / Helper Text:** What specific milestone or feature will you finish next? (e.g., "Integrate Bluetooth module and test mobile controller app").

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for the QP Chair

1. **Viewing At-Risk Teams in Google Sheets:**
   - Link your Google Form to a Google Spreadsheet (**Responses** tab > **Link to Sheets**).
   - Add conditional formatting on the **Showcase Confidence Level** column to highlight scores of `1` or `2` in red so you can reach out immediately on Discord.
2. **Flagging Mentor Requests:**
   - Filter Question 3.1 for any response other than "None" or "No".
   - Tag the team's private channel (`#team-X`) or notify mentors to drop by during Open Access Hours or Workathons.
